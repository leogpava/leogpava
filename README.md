## 👨‍💻 About me

I work at the intersection of **enterprise integration, cloud, and software engineering**, turning complex requirements into maintainable integration solutions.

- Pursuing a degree in **Computer Science**, strengthening my foundations in algorithms, data structures, and software engineering
- Building and evolving integration flows with **SAP Cloud Integration (CPI)**
- Designing synchronous and event-driven integrations
- Creating, securing, and governing APIs with **SAP API Management**
- Working with messaging and asynchronous architectures through **SAP Event Mesh**
- Drawing on previous hands-on study of **ABAP and SAP RAP** to better understand SAP backend development and end-to-end integration scenarios
- Automating transformations and integration logic with **Groovy, JavaScript, and Python**
- Exploring practical ways to bring **AI** into engineering workflows

I enjoy sharing what I learn, experimenting with new technologies, and making integration landscapes easier to understand and operate.
